Maison RHYVÉR was founded in Al Hoceima — where the Mediterranean meets the mountains of the Rif. Our fragrances are born from a mythology of memory: each one is a story, a landscape, a name.

The Lighnd Collection — ⵍⵉⵖⵏⴷ in Tifinagh script — is our first chapter. Ten Intense Eau de Parfums, each composed to endure through time.
