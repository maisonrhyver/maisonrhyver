ⵉⵜⵀⵔⵉ Heritage

The Root
of RHYVÉR.

RHYVÉR takes its name from Rif and ver — the old root for flow. A current that moves without losing its source.

It was founded in Al Hoceima as the first luxury maison to rise from the Rif — a region that had never, until now, produced a house of its own. Its identity is Riffian and Amazigh at the root: written first in that language, and only translated after, into the vocabulary the rest of the world already knows.

The idea is older than the House. In 1996, the founder's father established ITHRI, a heritage club built to preserve Riffian culture — a culture shaped by mountains, by coastline, by craftsmanship passed down rather than taught. Decades later, that inheritance became his son's to carry.

Today the same current runs through Riflands, and through scent. RHYVÉR does not borrow the Rif's identity. It continues it — still, and always, on its own terms.
